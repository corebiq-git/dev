Privacy Policy

This policy explains how COREBIQ handles information when you use the COREBIQ workspace and its modules.

App version 1.0Effective: 23 November 2026Owner: Corebiq Technologies · CM Innovations

1. Scope and responsibility

COREBIQ is a Health industry workspace and business operations platform developed and maintained by Corebiq Technologies under CM Innovations. This policy applies to the COREBIQ application, its modules, local settings, and related support services.

If your organisation enters patient, employee, customer, financial, or other regulated information, your organisation remains responsible for deciding whether COREBIQ is suitable for that information and for configuring access lawfully.

2. Information we process

Account and access information

We may process administrator email addresses, authentication identifiers, role assignments, module permissions, and sign-in or security events needed to operate the workspace.

Workspace records

Depending on the modules you use, your organisation may store company, branch, user, employee, customer, ledger, voucher, inventory, template, and operational records. The organisation that controls the workspace determines the purpose and content of those records.

Settings and device information

COREBIQ stores preferences such as language, country, currency, date format, typography, developer defaults, and access settings. Some preferences are stored locally in the browser. The app may also receive basic browser, device, and technical information needed for reliability and security.

3. How information is used

  • To authenticate users and enforce administrator-selected permissions.
  • To provide, maintain, troubleshoot, and secure COREBIQ modules.
  • To save, display, search, export, or otherwise process records at the workspace user’s request.
  • To communicate service, support, security, or policy information.
  • To prevent misuse, unauthorised access, fraud, and harmful activity.

We do not sell workspace records or use them for advertising. We do not resell the COREBIQ application.

4. Storage, providers, and sharing

COREBIQ may use infrastructure and service providers such as authentication, database, hosting, analytics-free application delivery, email, and support providers. Providers receive only the information needed to perform their service and are expected to protect it under their applicable terms.

Information may be shared with workspace administrators, authorised users, service providers, professional advisers where necessary, or authorities when required by law or to protect rights and safety. We do not share data for third-party advertising.

5. Retention, security, and your choices

Workspace data is retained while the workspace is active or as needed for the purposes described here. Administrators can manage users, roles, records, and access within the application. You may request correction, export, or deletion through your workspace administrator or by contacting us. Backups and legal obligations may require limited residual retention.

We use access controls, authenticated services, local-only developer administration tools, and reasonable technical safeguards. No internet or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

6. Children, changes, and contact

COREBIQ is a business workspace and is not directed to children. We may update this policy when the application, law, or processing practices change. The current version and effective date are shown on this page.
